# Prohibited activities

High-risk and prohibited business activities at BlindPay, and the disclosure obligations required during onboarding and ongoing monitoring.

Source: https://blindpay.com/docs/kb/prohibited-activities

## Summary

All customers must fully disclose their business activities to BlindPay during onboarding and ongoing monitoring. High-risk activities (such as licensed money services or VASPs) are allowed with disclosure, Enhanced Due Diligence, and ongoing monitoring. Prohibited activities are never supported under any circumstances. Failure to disclose relevant activities may result in onboarding rejection, account suspension, or termination.

## High-Risk Business Activities

**Allowed with disclosure, risk assessment, and enhanced controls.**

These activities are considered high risk. Being in one of these categories does not automatically disqualify you, but requires enhanced due diligence and ongoing monitoring. You must disclose these activities during onboarding. This list is not exhaustive:

- Licensed and disclosed money services, payment processing, or funds transmission (MSBs, PSPs, P2P platforms, prepaid/gift cards, ATMs)
- Virtual asset service providers (VASPs), including crypto exchanges, wallet providers, custody or escrow services, and stablecoin issuers
- Securities brokers or crowdfunding platforms
- Businesses with complex or multi-layered ownership structures

## Prohibited Business Activities

**Not supported under any circumstances.**

BlindPay does not provide services to businesses engaged in the following activities, directly or indirectly, regardless of jurisdiction or licensing claims.

| Category | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Adult Content and Sexually Oriented Services** | Businesses offering or facilitating sexually explicit, pornographic, obscene, or adult-oriented content or services, including dating, matchmaking, escort, and mail-order bride services. |
| **Drugs, Controlled Substances, Alcohol and Pseudo-Pharmaceuticals** | Sale, distribution, or facilitation of illegal, controlled, or unregulated substances and related paraphernalia, including unlicensed pharmaceuticals, substances designed to mimic illegal drugs, marijuana and related services, online pharmacies, supplement providers, and weight loss or diet products. |
| **Weapons, Ammunition, Explosives and Hazardous Materials** | Entities trading or dealing in weapons, firearms, ammunition, explosives, or related parts; espionage equipment, signal jammers, or signal blockers; and poisonous or hazardous materials. |
| **Gambling, Betting and Games of Chance** | Any business involving gambling, gaming, betting, or lotteries, whether licensed or unlicensed. |
| **Financial Crime-Linked Businesses and Fraudulent Models** | Ponzi schemes, pyramid schemes, multi-level marketing (MLM), fraud, shell banks, or any unlawful, abusive, or fraudulent business model. |
| **Unlicensed Money Services and Nested Payments** | Undisclosed or unlicensed money services businesses (MSBs), unlicensed money transmission, check cashing services, and nested banking or nested payment processing that is non-transparent or unlicensed. |
| **Hate, Violence, Terrorism and Discriminatory Activity** | Activities or materials promoting or enabling harm, hate, violence, misinformation, exploitation, or terrorism, including known terrorist organizations. |
| **Sanctioned and Illicit Entities** | Entities or individuals on OFAC, EU, UN, or other sanctions lists, or operating from prohibited jurisdictions under comprehensive embargoes. |
| **Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)** | Businesses where a principal owner or controlling person is a PEP. Identified associations are declined during onboarding. |
| **Identity Fraud, Anonymity Misuse and Shell Structures** | Anonymous or fictitious accounts, bearer share companies, non-operating companies, special purpose vehicles (SPVs), or entities designed to obscure beneficial ownership. |
| **High-Risk Lending, Debt and Predatory Financial Services** | Predatory, unfair, deceptive, or non-compliant financial products and practices, including abusive lending, credit repair services, bail bonds, collection agencies, and alimony or child support collection. |
| **Governmental, Diplomatic and Political Entities** | Government bodies, foreign governments, state-owned enterprises, embassies, consulates, supranational organizations, diplomatic entities, and political action groups. |
| **Data Misuse and Consumer Privacy Violations** | Businesses that compromise customer data or privacy. |
| **Intellectual Property Infringement and Counterfeit Goods** | Entities selling, distributing, or enabling IP-infringing or counterfeit products, including downloading, streaming, or distributing illegal or protected digital content. |
| **Wildlife, Human Remains and Restricted Biological Products** | Trade in endangered or protected animals and wildlife products, and human remains or body parts. |
| **Reproductive and Sensitive Health Services** | Abortion-related services and surrogacy services. |
| **Telemarketing and High-Risk Consumer Sales** | Telemarketing or direct marketing businesses, and timeshares or vacation property sales. |
| **Tobacco and Tobacco-Related Products** | Manufacture, distribution, or sale of tobacco products and related services. |
| **Charities and NGOs** | Non-governmental organizations, charitable foundations, and nonprofit entities. |
| **Legal, Notarial and Professional Services** | Lawyers, notaries, trustees, accountants and accounting firms that receive, hold or move funds on behalf of third parties, including through IOLTA, client trust, escrow or pooled client accounts. |
| **Banking and Correspondent Entities** | Shell banks, foreign banks, offshore banks, private banking, correspondent accounts, payable-through accounts, and concentration accounts. |
| **Retail, Hospitality and Consumer Goods** | Liquor stores, convenience stores, restaurants, and jewelry or precious metals dealers. |
| **Investment, Financial Advisory and Asset Management** | Financial advisory, retirement, and investment services; non-deposit investment products; trust and asset management services; and trade finance activities. This includes family offices and companies set up to hold or manage their owners’ own assets through a corporate vehicle (e.g., personal holding companies for investments, real estate, art, or collectibles). |
| **Construction** | Construction companies, general contractors, and related service providers. |

## Consequences of Violation

If prohibited activity is identified:

- **Account Review**: The account will be reviewed and subject to suspension or termination
- **Fund Freezing**: Funds may be frozen pending investigation
- **Legal Notification**: Relevant partners and authorities may be notified in accordance with legal and contractual obligations

## Reporting Violations

Report any prohibited activities on the platform to [compliance@blindpay.com](mailto:compliance@blindpay.com).
